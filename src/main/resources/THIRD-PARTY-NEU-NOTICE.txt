The SweatsEnhancements GUI settings were adapted from the Smooth AOTE section of ItemOverlays.java in the same upstream version. Packet confirmation timing was informed by MixinNetHandlerPlayClient.java from that version.

SweatsEnhancements modifications (2026): retained only relevant teleport visual motion (AOTE/AOTV/AOTL, merged Etherwarp and scroll-confirmed Shadow Warp), replaced item/config lookups with SweatsEnhancements's, removed unrelated abilities/cape/FOV features and a render-last redirect that conflicts with another installed renderer mixin, limited animation to server-confirmed movement following a matching item use, reset on world unload and disabled state, added separate GUI controls and guards.
